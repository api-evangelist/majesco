# Majesco

> Majesco is a leading cloud-native insurance technology company providing intelligent core software, digital solutions, analytics, and risk & compliance tools for Property & Casualty (P&C) and Life, Annuity & Health (L&AH) insurers worldwide. Serving 130+ global insurance carriers, Majesco's platform unifies Policy, Billing, and Claims with embedded GenAI (Majesco Copilot), Agentic AI, and real-time analytics — empowering insurers to modernize, innovate, and grow. Majesco is recognized as a QKS Group SPARK Matrix Leader for P&C and Life Insurance Policy Administration.
